Ragdoll Foundry

A physics sandbox. Pick something on the left, click the floor to place it, then grab, wire, weld and blow things up.

Drag
Grab anything. Drag empty space to pan. Wheel or pinch to zoom.
F
Use the held or hovered item: fire guns, arm explosives, toggle motors and switches.
A / D or Q / E
Rotate what you're holding. It keeps that angle until you let go. X flips it.
P
Pin the held point in place (press again on a pin to release).
Wire
Click two devices. Buttons, switches, timers and pressure plates power everything they're wired to.
Rope / spring
Click two points. Click empty space as the second point to tie to the world.
Hinge / weld
Click where two objects overlap to join them. On a single object, joins it to the world.
Heal
Restores a person: blood, wounds and broken bones (lost limbs stay lost).
Delete
Click to remove. The Delete key removes whatever you're pointing at.
Keys
1–0 tools, Space pause, T slow-mo, Ctrl+Z undo, Esc cancel. Right-drag pans.
